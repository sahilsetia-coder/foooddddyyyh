IMPORTANT FOOD-SAFETY NOTE

Don’t taste food to test it.

Visible mold can indicate spoilage, but harmful microbes may be present without visible signs. For soft, moist foods (such as berries and bread), discard the affected food rather than cutting around mold. A visual image cannot establish that food is safe to eat.
